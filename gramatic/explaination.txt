How to Do the 1-Hour Grammar Block (Step-by-Step)

Here is exactly how to divide your 60 minutes for Topic #1: Passive Voice:

    Minutes 00–15 (Learn the Formula): Read the rule below and understand why and how it works.

    Minutes 15–30 (Writing - Active Assembly): Write 3 sentences in your notebook or editor based on the exercise below.

    Minutes 30–45 (Out-Loud Drills): Read your 3 sentences out loud 5 times each, focusing on natural rhythm and pronunciation.

    Minutes 45–60 (Free Speaking): Explain a recent tech task out loud to yourself or an AI assistant, forcing yourself to use the passive voice 2 or 3 times.
